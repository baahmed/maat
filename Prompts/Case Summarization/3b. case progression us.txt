                          U.S. enforcement has no single spine. First identify which track the case
                          is on from the caption, the charging document, and the forum, then follow
                          that track. Cases can run on two tracks at once (e.g., an FTC Part 3
                          proceeding alongside a §13(b) injunction suit, or a DOJ criminal case
                          alongside follow-on private class actions).
